How to Make Your Standee NFC Compliant

Patients simply tap their iPhone or Android on the standee to open the review flow.

1

Order NFC Tags or NFC Acrylic Stands

Buy NTAG213 / NTAG215 / NTAG216 stickers or acrylic stands (costs ₹50 - ₹150 on Amazon India or local signage printers).

2

Download Free App: "NFC Tools"

Available free on both Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

3

Write Your Feedback Link into the Tag

  1. Open NFC Tools on your phone.
  2. Tap Write ➔ Add a record ➔ Custom URL / URI.
  3. Paste your live feedback URL (e.g. http://localhost:3000).
  4. Tap Write / 1 Byte and touch the back of your phone to the NFC chip. Done!
4

Affix Tag to Your Reception Standee

Stick the tag directly behind the QR code on your acrylic standee. Now your clinic standee supports BOTH Tap (NFC) and Scan (QR)!
